Bazar Dor

A daily market price tracker for essential goods in Bangladesh.

Main Technology Stack

Next.js, React, Tailwind CSS, Vercel (Hosting)

Brief Description

Bazar Dor is a web application that helps users check the daily prices of essential commodities like rice, lentils, oil, vegetables, fish, and meat in Bangladesh. It aims to provide a simple and reliable way for people to stay informed about market rates.

Challenges Faced

One of the main challenges was sourcing accurate and up-to-date price data for various products across different regions. Another challenge was designing a clean, user-friendly interface that makes it easy for users to quickly find the information they need.

Potential Improvements & Future Plans

  • Integrate a real-time API to fetch prices automatically.
  • Add user accounts to allow people to save their favorite products.
  • Implement a price comparison feature to show the cheapest options.
  • Add a feature to filter prices by location or region.
